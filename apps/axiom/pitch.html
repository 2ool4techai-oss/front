Chain Lock-In (The Deepest)
Once robot companies record on our chain, every record references the previous. You cannot migrate history to a competing chain — the chain IS the proof. Switching means losing the entire legal and insurance record of the robot's life.

Standard-Setting (The Most Durable)
The first entity to define the benchmark metric owns it permanently. Waymo's disengagement rate is now industry law — not because regulators created it, but because Waymo published it first. We publish the AXIOM score. The market adopts it.

More robots record on the chain → richer actuarial data → better trust scores → more enterprise mandates → more robots join. Each new robot makes the platform more valuable for every existing participant.
